# Code of Conduct

This project is for people learning, and people learning ask basic questions. Please help make that comfortable.

**Expected:**

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with people working in their second (or third) language.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.

**Not accepted:**

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background or experience level.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Continuing to engage with someone who has asked you to stop.
